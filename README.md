The objective of this project was to establish a direct partnership with the football school of Pindamonhangaba in order to meet the requirements requested for the development of a customized website. Using PHP and MySQL technologies, the site was designed to offer specific features according to the institution's needs, such as information management, a match calendar, as well as a news and contact section. The proposal aimed to provide an efficient, functional, and easily accessible digital solution for both the school's administrators and for students and their parents or guardians.
